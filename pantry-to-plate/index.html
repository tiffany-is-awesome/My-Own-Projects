How it works

My kitchen

Everything you have, grouped by what needs using first. Tap any food to change its amount, place or dates.

Times are cautious estimates to help you plan, not guarantees. A printed use-by date always wins, and how food was stored matters. If in doubt, throw it out.

Check every spot

Food hiding at the back is what gets forgotten. Open each spot and tap what you find.

Always in my kitchen

Tick the basics you always keep. Recipes assume you have enough of them.

Cook something

Dependable recipes made only from what's in your kitchen. Taste comes first, then using up what needs using.

Meal planner

Plan a day or a week from what you have. Each meal gets its own share of your food, and the shopping list only shows what's genuinely missing.

Rescue my food

Food that needs using soon, and the best ways to save it: cook it, freeze it, preserve it or turn it into something new. Food that isn't safe is clearly marked.

Saved recipes

Everything you've saved, in one place. Search it, sort it into collections, and tick what you'll cook this week.
